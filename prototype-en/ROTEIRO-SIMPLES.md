# CogniMente: simple presentation script

**Tempo:** cerca de 5 minutos falando devagar.
**Link:** https://cognimente-en.vercel.app
**Como ler:** o texto em inglês é a fala. O que está em *[itálico]* é o que fazer na tela.
**Dica:** frases curtas, uma ideia por vez. Pode fazer pausas, é normal.

---

## 1. Hello and the problem (40 seconds)

*[Fique na página inicial, sem clicar.]*

> Hello everyone. My name is Lucca, and this is **CogniMente**.
>
> People are living longer. And to age well, it is important to keep the mind active.
>
> But most brain games are not made for older people. The letters are small. The games are fast and confusing.
>
> Also, many families live far away. They don't know how their parents or grandparents are doing.

## 2. The idea (20 seconds)

> CogniMente is a brain-training app **for older adults**.
>
> The games are simple. The buttons are big. And the family can follow everything.
>
> Just ten minutes a day.

## 3. Try it (30 seconds)

*[Jogue o "Try it now" e encontre os 3 pares.]*

> You can try it right here, before you sign up.
>
> I tap two cards... I find a pair... and I finish.
>
> That's it. Every game is this simple.

## 4. Sign up and home screen (1 minute)

*[Role para o topo, clique em "Start for free", digite uma senha qualquer com 6 letras e clique em "Create free account".]*

> To start, you only need a name, an email and a password.

*[No dashboard:]*

> This is the home screen. Our example user is Margaret.
>
> Here she sees her **streak**: how many days in a row she played.
>
> And here is **Today's plan**. The app chooses the next game and the level for her. She just presses Start.

*[Role até "Text size" e clique em "A++". Depois clique em "A".]*

> She can also make the text bigger with one tap.

## 5. Play a game (1 minute)

*[Clique em "Games", depois "Quick Math", depois "Easy". Responda 2 ou 3 contas. Erre uma de propósito.]*

> We have four games: memory, math, family faces and words.
>
> The buttons are big, and there is time to think.
>
> If she makes a mistake, the app shows the right answer. No stress.

*[Termine o jogo.]*

> At the end, she sees her points and her progress.

## 6. Family Faces (40 seconds)

*[Clique em "Family".]*

> This is my favorite part. Margaret can add photos of her family.

*[Clique em "Games", depois "Family Faces", depois "Easy". Jogue uma rodada.]*

> Then the app asks: "Who is this?" She sees her grandson and chooses his name.
>
> It trains the memory, and it uses people she loves.

## 7. Health and family report (50 seconds)

*[Clique em "Health".]*

> The app also cares about the body. Every day she can say how much water she drank, how she slept and how she feels.

*[Clique em "Progress", depois em "Preview report".]*

> Here she sees her progress during the week.
>
> And every Sunday, her family gets a short report like this one. So her daughter knows she is doing well, even from far away.

## 8. End (30 seconds)

*[Volte para a página inicial e role até "Start today."]*

> So, CogniMente is **simple** for older adults, **personal** with their own family, and it keeps the family **close**.
>
> This is a prototype with example data. Our next step is to build the full version in English.
>
> Thank you very much! Any questions?

---

## Se fizerem perguntas (respostas curtas)

**How does the level change?**
> The app looks at the last games. If she does well, the level goes up. If not, it goes down.

**Is it scientific?**
> Keeping the mind active is good for healthy aging. But the app is not a medical treatment.

**How will you make money?**
> The games are free. Later, we can have a paid plan for families.

**What about privacy?**
> The photos and data belong to the user. Only the family members she chooses get the report.

**Não entendi a pergunta:**
> Sorry, can you repeat the question, please?
