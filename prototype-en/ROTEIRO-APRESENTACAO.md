# CogniMente: presentation script (English)

**Duração estimada:** 5 a 6 minutos
**Como usar:** o texto em inglês é o que você fala. As instruções em *[colchetes e itálico]* estão em português e dizem o que fazer na tela.
**Antes de começar:** abra o protótipo na landing page, com zoom do navegador em 100%. Se for apresentar pelo celular, abra o link no celular e espelhe a tela.

---

## 1. Opening: the problem (about 45 seconds)

*[Fique na landing page, no topo. Não clique em nada ainda.]*

> Good morning, everyone. My name is Lucca, and today I want to show you **CogniMente**.
>
> Let me start with a simple fact. People are living longer than ever, and staying mentally active is one of the best things we can do as we age. Regular cognitive exercise helps keep memory, attention and reasoning sharp.
>
> The problem is that most brain-training apps are not made for older adults. The text is small, the games are fast and complicated, and the whole experience feels like it was designed for a twenty-year-old.
>
> And there's a second problem. Their families often live far away and have no simple way to know how their parents or grandparents are doing day to day.

## 2. The solution (about 30 seconds)

*[Aponte para o título "Exercise your mind. Live better."]*

> CogniMente is a brain-training app designed **specifically for older adults**. Simple games, large buttons, calm design, and no pressure.
>
> Our idea is: **ten minutes a day, at your own pace**, and the family stays connected along the way.

## 3. Live demo: try it on the landing page (about 30 seconds)

*[Jogue o mini jogo "Try it now" ao vivo. Encontre os 3 pares.]*

> Before I even sign up, I can try it right here. This is a small version of our Memory Match game. I just tap two cards, find the matching pair... and that's it.
>
> *[Quando terminar:]* That's exactly how simple every exercise feels. No tutorial needed.

*[Role a página devagar mostrando "How it works" e a prévia do relatório para a família.]*

> It works in three steps: create a profile, play a short session, and your family receives a weekly report. I'll come back to that report in a moment.

## 4. Sign in and the home screen (about 1 minute)

*[Role para o topo e clique em "Start for free". Os dados já vêm preenchidos. Digite qualquer senha com 6 caracteres e clique em "Create free account".]*

> Signing up takes less than a minute. Name, email, password. Done.

*[No dashboard:]*

> This is the home screen. Meet Margaret, our example user.
>
> At the top she sees her **day streak**, which keeps her motivated to come back.
>
> Right below is **Today's plan**. The app picks the next game for her and **the right difficulty level based on her recent results**. She doesn't need to think about settings. She just presses Start.

*[Role até o fim do dashboard e clique em "A++" no Text size.]*

> And this is one detail I'm proud of. With one tap, Margaret can make all the text larger. Accessibility is built in from the start.

*[Clique em "A" para voltar ao tamanho normal.]*

## 5. Playing a game (about 1 minute)

*[Clique em "Start" no Today's plan, ou vá em Games e escolha "Quick Math".]*

> We have four games, and each one trains a different area of the mind: memory, reasoning, emotional memory and language.

*[Na escolha de dificuldade, aponte para o selo "Suggested for you".]*

> There are three levels, and the app shows which one it recommends.

*[Escolha "Easy" e responda 2 ou 3 contas. Erre uma de propósito.]*

> The timer is generous, the buttons are large, and when she makes a mistake the app gently shows the right answer. There's no punishment, just learning.

*[Termine o jogo. Se acertar 80% ou mais, aparecem confetes.]*

> At the end she sees her score, accuracy, and a friendly message. And if she does really well, the app suggests moving up a level next time.

## 6. Family Faces: the emotional part (about 45 seconds)

*[Vá em "Family" no menu.]*

> This is my favorite feature. Margaret, or her children, can add photos of real family members.

*[Vá em Games → "Family Faces" → "Easy" e jogue uma rodada.]*

> Then the app turns them into a game: "Who is this?" She sees her grandson's photo and picks his name. It exercises memory, but it's also emotional. It's her own family.

## 7. Health and the family report (about 1 minute)

*[Vá em "Health".]*

> CogniMente takes care of the whole person, not only the mind. Every day Margaret can log how much water she drank, how many hours she slept, her mood and her physical activity. It takes about fifteen seconds.

*[Vá em "Progress". Mostre o gráfico e clique em "Preview report".]*

> Here she can follow her progress week by week.
>
> And here is the report her family receives every Sunday: days played, accuracy, usual mood, and simple insights like "Math accuracy went up twelve percent this week."
>
> For a daughter who lives in another city, this is real peace of mind.

## 8. Closing (about 30 seconds)

*[Volte para a landing page, na seção final "Start today."]*

> To sum up: CogniMente is **simple** for older adults, **personal** because it uses their own family, and **connected** because it keeps loved ones informed.
>
> What you saw today is a prototype with example data. The full version, with real accounts, scoring and adaptive difficulty, is already being built in Portuguese, and our next step is bringing it to English-speaking users.
>
> Thank you very much. I'm happy to take any questions.

## Possible questions and short answers

**"How does the adaptive difficulty work?"**
> The app looks at the accuracy of the last sessions in each game. If accuracy is high, it suggests a harder level. If it drops, it suggests an easier one. The goal is to keep the challenge comfortable, never frustrating.

**"Is there scientific evidence behind it?"**
> Research shows that staying mentally active is associated with healthier cognitive aging. CogniMente doesn't claim to treat or prevent any disease. It's a tool to keep the mind active every day.

**"How will you make money?"**
> The core games are free. We are considering a family plan with the weekly report and extra features for caregivers.

**"What about privacy? You're storing family photos."**
> Photos and health data belong to the user. They are used only inside the app, and the family report is sent only to the people the user chooses.

**"Why older adults and not everyone?"**
> Because they are the group that benefits most and is served least. Designing for them means large text, simple flows and no pressure. That focus is our difference.
